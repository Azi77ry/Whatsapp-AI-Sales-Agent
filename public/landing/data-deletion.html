Data Deletion Instructions

Last updated: 10 November 2026

If you are a business using Aziry Tech (Merchant)

  1. Log in to your dashboard at azirytech.online/dashboard.
  2. Open Settings and choose Delete account.
  3. Confirm with your password.

This permanently deletes your account and everything linked to it: products, customer conversations, orders, special requests and WhatsApp connection details. It happens immediately and cannot be undone. Copies may remain in database backups for a limited period until those backups are overwritten.

To disconnect WhatsApp without deleting your account, use Disconnect in the WhatsApp Connections section; the stored access token is revoked.

If you messaged a business on WhatsApp (Customer)

  • To stop reminder messages, reply STOP to the business. Reply START to receive them again.
  • To have your messages and order details deleted, ask the business directly, or email azirytech@gmail.com with the subject "Data deletion request", the WhatsApp number you used and the name of the business.

We will confirm your request and delete the data within 30 days, unless we must keep some of it to meet a legal obligation. We may ask you to confirm that the number belongs to you.

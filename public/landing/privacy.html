Privacy Policy

Last updated: 10 November 2026

This policy explains how Aziry Tech ("we", "us") handles personal data in the Aziry Tech WhatsApp AI Sales Agent (the "Service"): a platform that lets businesses ("Merchants") answer their customers on WhatsApp with an AI assistant, manage products and take orders.

Two kinds of people use the Service. Merchants create an account with us. Customers are people who message a Merchant on WhatsApp. For customer conversations, the Merchant decides why and how the data is used; we process it on the Merchant's behalf to provide the Service.

1. Data we collect

From Merchants

  • Account details: business name, email address, phone number and a password (stored only as a one-way hash).
  • Business content: products, prices, images, business description, payment instructions and settings.
  • WhatsApp connection details: WhatsApp Business Account ID, phone number ID and the access token issued by Meta.
  • Billing records: subscription payments made through our payment provider (we do not store mobile-money PINs).

From Customers who message a Merchant

  • WhatsApp phone number and WhatsApp profile name.
  • Message content sent to and from the Merchant's WhatsApp number, including text, image captions and transcripts of voice notes.
  • Order and request details the customer provides, such as name, delivery address, product, size and colour.
  • Message status (sent, delivered, read) and preferences such as opting out with "STOP".

Voice notes are downloaded only to convert them to text and are then discarded; we keep the transcript, not the audio. Images sent by customers are not stored by us.

2. How we use data

  • To read customer messages and generate replies with AI on the Merchant's behalf.
  • To search the Merchant's products, create orders and special requests, and send order updates.
  • To show conversations, orders and reports to the Merchant in their dashboard, and to let the Merchant reply personally.
  • To respect customer choices: talking to a person instead of the AI, and opting out of reminders.
  • To keep the Service secure, prevent abuse and spam, and meet legal obligations.
  • To bill Merchants and send account emails.

We do not sell personal data, we do not use it for advertising, and we do not use customer conversations to train our own AI models.

4. Security

  • Data is sent over encrypted connections (HTTPS).
  • Message content and WhatsApp access tokens are encrypted in our database (AES-256-GCM). Passwords are stored as hashes.
  • Each Merchant can only access their own data.

Please note: this is not end-to-end encryption. To provide the Service, our systems decrypt messages to generate AI replies and to show them to the Merchant, and message text is sent to the AI providers above. Access by our staff is limited to what is needed for support, security and legal reasons.

5. How long we keep data

  • Conversations, orders and products are kept while the Merchant's account is active, so the Merchant can see their history.
  • When a Merchant deletes their account, all of its data, including customer conversations and orders, is deleted from our database.
  • Deleted data may remain in database backups for a limited period until those backups are overwritten.

6. Your choices and rights

  • Customers can reply STOP to stop reminder messages, ask to talk to a person, and ask the Merchant or us to access or delete their data.
  • Merchants can view, edit and export their data in the dashboard and delete their account at any time.
  • Depending on where you live, including under Tanzania's Personal Data Protection Act, 2022, you may have rights to access, correct, delete or object to the processing of your data.

See Data Deletion Instructions for how to request deletion.

7. Children

The Service is for businesses and is not directed at children. Merchants must be at least 18 years old.

8. Changes

We may update this policy. We will change the date above and, for significant changes, notify Merchants in the dashboard or by email.
